Our story

Founded by a farmer's son in Brittany

Esteban Massue grew up on a Breton farm in Treffendel, Ille-et-Vilaine.

At 21, he founded Breizh Agrimat to help farmers sell their used equipment — like a real estate agent for tractors.

After two years, 1,000+ machines sold and recognition from major French agricultural press, he's now scaling the model across Europe.
